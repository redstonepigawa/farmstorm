### 0.3 How to judge that a phase is complete

All three gates must be green to pass; missing one is handled as "not done":

1. **Exit criteria**: every item of §x.4 can be pointed to an assertion output of `logs/latest.log`; the return values of `/execute` `/data get` `/setblock` `/loot` `/clear` in game; the on-screen readings of F3 / F3+B; the content of `config/farmstorm-server.toml` or of a file under `src/generated/resources`; the artifacts in `build/libs` and `dist` (including the entry list of `jar tf`). Structural evidence (0 `grep` hits, an empty `git diff`, a byte-by-byte PNG comparison, the `jar tf` entry list) is used only in the cases item 4 of `25` §0.3 allows. Saying "implemented but not verified" does not pass.
2. **Task coverage**: every F-xx in §x.3 has evidence that can be pointed to; when the agent skipped one, require it to be made up per the definition of done of `26` — do not fill it in yourself.
3. **Repository state**: `.tools\gw.cmd build` exits 0; `git status` shows only files inside this phase's boundary; 00 – 26 under `docs/zh` and all of `docs/en/`, the fields of the existing 22 `CropProfile` profiles, the defaults of the existing 155 config keys (except the 1 that F-139 authorises), the existing 18 advancements and the existing conversion recipes are untouched.

### 2.5 Common failure modes

1. **Putting F-139's 166 before F-141** (A-21 and G3 of `24` §1.4): the criterion of F-140 / F-141 is "**this task did not touch the expected config set**" — while F-139 has not landed the config section is **155 green**, and once it has landed it is a **166 `real_extra` red**; neither reading **constitutes a failure of this task**, and F-142 closes it out. What it turns into: the agent takes this red for a registry-surface regression and goes off to change the §2 section's counts, getting messier the more it changes; or, the other way round, it pulls out F-139's 11 keys in order to clear the red. Catching it early: first check whether the config section in the tool output of F-140 / F-141 "shows 11 extra keys" — if it does, F-139 has landed, this red belongs to F-142 to close out, and it is not charged to F-140 / F-141. Catching it early: F-140 and F-142 each carry an assertion that `git diff --stat docs/` is empty, so running it once before committing is enough.
4. **Taking the 12 rows for 12 new keys** (the note under the table of `24` §8.1): taking `melon_grenade.horizontalJitter` for a new key and registering it a second time, or checking the count by 12 and landing on 167 leaf nodes. ### 4.5 Common failure modes

1. **Trying to register a `GameRule` or writing "gamerule"** (A-19; RT-25): `GameRules.register` is `private static` and cannot be registered inside the no-Mixin red line; going through reflection or `Unsafe` crashes the server. What it turns into: it does not compile, or an `IllegalAccessException` when the server starts; worse still, a non-existent command name such as `/gamerule farmstorm:...` is left behind in the documents. Catching it early: `grep -rn "GameRules.register" src/main/java` gives 0; the word "gamerule" appears nowhere in the deliverables.
2. **Writing the command literal as `farmland_limit` or `farmlandLimit`** (D-116; `24` §7.2): Brigadier literals are case-sensitive and underscore-sensitive, and a typo means it cannot be completed. What it turns into: `/farmstorm` completion has no such item, while the feedback wording refers to it. 9. **Summing the quota by "the team's number of cores"** (D-117; `23` §11.4): implementing the team convention as "the team shares one core count". What it turns into: the quota does not follow a change of team, or one person leaving takes the whole team's quota with him. Catching it early: after a change of team the new teammates immediately count as allies and the quota is summed over the new team (§11.4); going offline does not reclaim already tilled farmland (§11.3).
10. **`countOwned` not doing the lazy check** (the supplement of D-122; `23` §11.2): a core cleared with `/setblock air` is still counted. ### 6.5 Common failure modes

1. **Making the language keys add up to the total without adding up item by item** (the three reconciliation formulas of `24` §9.4): the total key count reaches 315 but `farmstorm.configuration.*` is 165 or `command.*` is one short. What it turns into: the total is right and the structure wrong, and `configuration` no longer equals the config key count of 166. Catching it early: both formulas must hold — the six-item total `26 + 32 + 166 + 1 + 2 + 88 = 315` and the increment `1 + 1 + 11 + 1 + 2 + 0 = 16` — and then compare the `configuration` count against 166 on its own.
2. **Copying the shape of a block with no BlockItem for the loot table, or simply missing this one JSON** (A-27; `24` §2.3): the omission comes from the "4" of `23` §1.3 / §10.3. What it turns into: mining the core drops nothing while DataGen reports no error; there are only 4 JSONs and they do not match the accounting of 5. Catching it early: `loot_table/blocks/outpost_core.json` must exist and this row must state explicitly that the block has a `BlockItem`; `/loot give` and mining directly give the same product by both paths.
3. **Putting the core into the three `needs_*_tool` level tags** (item 2 of D-119; `24` §11 #11): adding a level requirement along the way "to make it look hard". What it turns into: a wooden pickaxe mines out no drop, which conflicts directly with the "a wooden pickaxe is enough" acceptance; and `grep -c outpost_core` in the generated tags is non-zero. Catching it early: the `grep -c` of all three `needs_*_tool` is 0 and it goes only into `mineable/pickaxe`.
4. **Writing only "pass" in the acceptance record** (item 4 of F-170): writing two words in every cell after running the nine. What it turns into: "implemented but not verified", which the definition of done of `26` does not accept, and F-175's summary cannot be re-checked either. Catching it early: every cell of the "measured" column must carry a reading (a tick count, a block count, an effect list, `ItemDamage`, a packet volume, a coordinate), and the "evidence location" column must point to a log line or a command return value.
5. **Taking a known consequence of the boundary re-run for a defect and changing the code** (`23` §11.5 / §11.10 / §11.13): seeing "traps in unloaded chunks still take a slot" or "ally farmland in the overlap area is flattened as well" and changing the implementation. What it turns into: what gets changed is the acceptance criterion rather than the implementation, the test passes but the behaviour does not match the design; or, the other way round, code already accepted in Phase 22 / 23 is touched. Catching it early: the deliverable of F-172 / F-173 is a **record** (no code change); every known consequence must be named as a "known consequence" in the record and pointed to the section number of `23`.
6. **Running only 18 of the 31** (item 1 of F-175): running only `24` §11 and missing the 13 of `23` §11; or the other way round, running only the boundaries and not the self-checks. What it turns into: the summary table has only 18 rows and the accounting of `18 + 13 = 31` does not add up. Catching it early: the entry counts must add up to 31, the two batches having 18 / 13 rows, each with an evidence location.
7. **Passing an old artifact off as 0.1.0** (item 3 of F-174): `dist/` still holds the 0.0.4 jar, or both the old and the new build are in `build/libs`. What it turns into: the `version` in `neoforge.mods.toml` is not 0.1.0; the author takes the package, joins the server and the behaviour does not match. Catching it early: read the version line of `neoforge.mods.toml` with `jar tf`; only one jar may be in `build/libs`.
8. **One of the three red lines broken** (`24` §11 #18; D-40 / C-6): introducing a custom network packet "to send only to ally players", or introducing a Mixin "to register a `GameRule`". What it turns into: one of the three `grep -c` is non-zero and the engineering red lines of `26` fail the phase outright. Catching it early: all three `grep` are run once in #18 of F-171; the boundary particles must go through the vanilla `ClientboundLevelParticlesPacket`.
